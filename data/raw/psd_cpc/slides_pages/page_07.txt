Key Quotes & Statistics from State Demographer Dr. Kate Watkins presented on December 9, 
2025 to PSD Board of Education

●
Statewide School-Age Peak & Decline (Ages 5–17):
“We anticipate that statewide, the school-age population (those 5 to 17 years old) reached a 
near-term peak in 2020 at 947,912. Since then, Colorado has averaged an estimated 5,900 
decline each year in the school-age population. Declines in this population are projected to 
continue through 2031.”

●
Local Household Proportion:
○
Dr. Watkins highlighted that just 23% of all households in Fort Collins contain 
school-aged (K–12) children.

Regional Context & Drivers Shared During the Meeting

●
Larimer County Downgrade: The State Demography Office downgraded Larimer County’s 
long-term total population forecast by approximately 35,900 people through 2050.
