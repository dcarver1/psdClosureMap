Fewer, Fuller Schools Means More 
Equitable Opportunities for All Students

Impact of Enrollment Trends Principal Feedback

●
Erosion of Essential Support Services: Principals report that declining 
enrollment has already forced significant cuts to student direct supports, 
including paraprofessionals, interventionists, and mental health counselors.

●
Reduced Programming and Increased Ratios: Budget constraints are 
leading to higher student-to-teacher ratios and the reduction of "Specials" 
(Art, Music, PE) and Gifted & Talented (GT) programming.

●
The "Growth" Advantage: Conversely, principals indicated that increased 
enrollment would allow them to stabilize staffing, expand specialized 
services, and provide more robust educational offerings for all students.
