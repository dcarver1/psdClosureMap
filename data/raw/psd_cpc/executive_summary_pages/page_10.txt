3. Enrollment Projections: Longitudinal demographic analysis and multi-year student enrollment forecasts 
across feeder areas. 
• 
Student Enrollment at Johnson Elementary School is projected at 266 students in the 2026-2027 
school year and is expected to remain in continuous decline for the next five years, losing up to 54 
students, 20.3%, by the 2030-2031 school year. At this time, we expect a funded enrollment of 
approximately 212 students during the 2030-2031 school year. 

• 
Johnson Elementary has a choice-out rate of approximately 30% and a choice-in rate of 37%, 
resulting in modest net choice-in. While choice helps support enrollment, the school remains 
affected by the broader decline in the elementary-aged population across the surrounding 
neighborhoods. 

B. Implementation Criteria (Operational Viability & Transition Management) 

4. Traffic, Safety, and Walkability: Management of vehicular traffic flow, pedestrian safety, and 
neighborhood walkability for students and families. 

• 
Increased traffic is expected around McGraw and Olander with increased enrollment; however, no 
structural traffic mitigation measures are recommended. 

• 
PSD and the City of Fort Collins recognize there will be increased delays around the school during 
15-minute peak periods at pick-up and drop-off times. 

• 
McGraw is within the City of Fort Collins and has adequate curb space and sidewalk connectivity 
within walk boundaries. While no infrastructure changes are needed, Hinsdale is a narrow street 
with limited access off of Harmony Rd. Increased attendance will further congest Hinsdale Rd. into 
McGraw. 

• 
Olander is within the City of Fort Collins and has ample curb space and sidewalk connectivity within 
walk boundaries. 

• 
Closest SRO (FCPS at Webber) is 1.8 miles from McGraw. Closest fire station (PFA Station 5) is 1.2 
miles from school.
