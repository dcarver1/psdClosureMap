2. Facility Condition Assessment and Quality: Evaluation of physical accessibility, security infrastructure, 
repairability, physical plant needs, and outdoor recreational space. 

 

• 
ADA upgrades pose cost and feasibility challenges due to the age and structural design of the 
building. Additionally, classroom sizes do not meet the standard square footage size (700 SF) used 
for classrooms in modern structures. The overall age and structural design of the building create 
significant challenges for expansion and modernization. 

• 
Repairability and deferred maintenance costs at Timnath are estimated at $6,456,149. This amount 
does not account for the added financial needs that will be necessary for ADA upgrades triggered by 
deferred maintenance construction. 

• 
Building does not have an elevator, building does not have fully ADA accessible routes, building is 
not air conditioned, site does not have braille signage, building needs internal LED lighting upgrades 
and partial external LED lighting upgrades. 

• 
This site contains Walt Weiss baseball field which was donated to PSD by the Colorado Rockies 
organization. This donation would have to be addressed with future use. 

 

3. Enrollment Projections: Longitudinal demographic analysis and multi-year student enrollment forecasts 
across feeder areas. 
• 
Student Enrollment at Timnath Elementary School is expected to have a relatively minor decline of 
415 students in 2026–27 to 407 students in 2029–30. 

• 
Compared to the rest of Poudre School District, the Timnath area is expected to show modest gains 
over the next five years. Choice out of Timnath is 35% and choice in is 5%. This is the highest choice 
out rate of the three Timnath area elementary schools.
