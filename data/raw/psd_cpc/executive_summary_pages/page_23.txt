B. Implementation Criteria (Operational Viability & Transition Management) 

1. Traffic, Safety, and Walkability: Management of vehicular traffic flow, pedestrian safety, and 
neighborhood walkability for students and families. 

Bamford/Linton 
• 
Increased traffic is expected around Bamford with increased enrollment; however, no structural 
traffic mitigation measures are recommended. 

• 
Increased traffic is expected around Linton with increased enrollment; however, no structural traffic 
mitigation measures are recommended. 

• 
PSD and the City of Loveland recognize there will be increased delays around the school during 15-
minute peak periods at pick-up and drop-off times. 

• 
Bamford: Closest SRO (LCSO at CLPMS) assigned is 17.4 miles from site. Loveland PD does respond 
to calls for service from Bamford, and Windsor PD would respond in an emergency. Closest fire 
station (Loveland Fire Station 10) is 5.0 miles from the site. However, Windsor-Severance Fire 
(Station 3) is also 1.4 miles from school. 

• 
Linton: Closest SRO (FCPS at Preston) assigned is 2.2 miles from site. Closest fire station (PFA 
Station 10) is 1.4 miles from school. 

 

2. Transportation & Commute Length: Availability of district transportation routes and total commute 
length for affected students. 
• 
9 students that previously walked to Timnath Elementary will be eligible for busing to their 
neighborhood school of Bamford or Linton. 

• 
Average student ride times to Timnath currently: 

o Average – 19 minutes 
o Max ride time – 40 minutes 

• 
Scenario student ride times to Bamford and Linton: 

o Average – 31 minutes 
o Max ride time – 50 minutes 

 

3. Maximization of School Program Offerings: Preservation and expansion of Specials (art, music, PE), 
electives, extracurriculars/athletics, early childhood programming, and before/after-school care. 
• 
We project Linton and Bamford to have 3-4 tracks at each grade level. This will facilitate full-time or 
nearly full-time specials at each school. 

• 
Before and after school care can be accommodated at the receiving site. 

• 
We are projecting that early childhood programming would remain at Linton and Bamford.
