3. Enrollment Projections: Longitudinal demographic analysis and multi-year student enrollment forecasts 
across feeder areas. 

 

• 
Blevins Middle School has experienced continuously declining enrollment. The 2021-2022 school 
year had the highest enrollment of the past 5 school years, 543 students. Blevins Middle School's 
student enrollment has declined by 178 students, 32.8%, to 365 students in the 2025-2026 school 
year. 

• 
Student Enrollment at Blevins Middle School is projected at 360 students in the 2026-2027 school 
year and is expected to remain in continuous decline for the next five years, losing up to 181 
students, 50.3%, by the 2030-2031 school year. At this time, we expect an enrollment of 
approximately 179 students in the 2030-2031 school year. 

• 
Blevins Middle School has a high choice-out rate of approximately 54%, compared with a 15%choice-
in rate, resulting in a significant net loss of students through school choice. This is compounded by 
declining enrollment among the elementary schools that feed into Blevins, creating continued 
pressure on the long-term feeder pipeline.
