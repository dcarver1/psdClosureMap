Summary Rationale 

Multiple scenarios were evaluated for the 5 southwest elementary schools (Beattie, Johnson, Lopez, McGraw, 
Olander). Johnson Elementary ranked 1st in the region (lowest enrollment, lowest utilization, lowest FCA) on 
composite z scores (-1.931) with the next closest school at (-1.743). After reviewing the identification criteria, 
implementation analysis, and equity analysis, the CPC determined that Johnson would be recommended due its' 
combined z-score, proximity to other elementary schools and how its' closure contributed to positively providing 
greater stability and programmatic opportunities for all students in the region. 

Financial Considerations: 
Cost for overall day-to-day operation could decrease by $1.89 million (includes estimate for Beattie closure as 
well). This includes changes to student-based budget, school administration, custodial, child nutrition, and 
utilities.
