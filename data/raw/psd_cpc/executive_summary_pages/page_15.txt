PSD Mountain Schools, which consist of Red Feather Elementary, Stove Prairie Elementary and 
Livermore Elementary serve small, geographically distinct mountain communities where enrollment 
is highly sensitive to changes in the number of families living within each community. Choice 
patterns vary considerably across the three schools. Because of the small student populations, the 
movement of only a few families or students can substantially affect both enrollment and choice 
rates from year to year. Long-term enrollment is therefore closely tied to the unique population and 
housing dynamics of these mountain communities. 

B. Implementation Criteria (Operational Viability & Transition Management) 

4. Traffic, Safety, and Walkability: Management of vehicular traffic flow, pedestrian safety, and 
neighborhood walkability for students and families. 

• 
Increased traffic is expected around CLPE with increased enrollment. 

• 
PSD and Larimer County recognize there will be increased delays around the receiving schools 
during 15-minute peak periods at pick-up and drop-off times. 

• 
CLPE is in Larimer County and has ample on property bus and car parking, and minimal 
infrastructure within walk boundaries. 

• 
Red Feather and Stove Prairie are more vulnerable to wildfire natural hazards than other PSD 
schools. 

• 
Emergency response time is improved, and natural hazard risks are reduced by moving the 
Mountain Schools to CLPE. An SRO (LCSO at CLPM) assigned to CLPE is stationed next door. The 
nearest fire station (PFA station 7) is .7 miles away. 

 

5. Transportation & Commute Length: Availability of district transportation routes and total commute 
length for affected students. 
• 
All students who are currently educated in the three mountain schools would be eligible for busing 
to CLPE. 

• 
All Mountain School students are currently bussed to school. 

•
