3. Enrollment Projections: Longitudinal demographic analysis and multi-year student enrollment forecasts 
across feeder areas. 

 

• 
Student enrollment at Beattie Elementary School is projected at 225 students in the 2026-2027 
school year and is expected to remain generally stable with a slight increase of 4 students, 1.8%, to 
229 students in 2030-2031. 

• 
Beattie Elementary has a choice-out rate of approximately 39%, compared with a 13% choice-in 
rate, resulting in the largest net choice-out pattern among the five southwest elementary schools. 
With significantly fewer students choosing into Beattie to offset those choosing out, enrollment is 
more dependent on the size of its neighborhood student population and is particularly sensitive to 
continued demographic decline in the surrounding area.
