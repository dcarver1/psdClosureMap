3. Enrollment Projections: Longitudinal demographic analysis and multi-year student enrollment forecasts 
across feeder areas. 
• 
The enrollment of PSD Mountain Schools, which consist of Red Feather Elementary, Stove Prairie 
Elementary and Livermore Elementary, is sensitive to small population changes within the mountain 
communities. As a whole the PSD Mountain Schools had a total enrollment of 109 students in the 
2021-2022 school year. The school populations have oscillated up and down reaching a low of 78 
students in the 2025-2026 school year. A net decline of 31 students, 28.4% over the 5 year period. 

• 
Student Enrollment in the PSD Mountain Schools is projected at 82 students total in the 2026-27 
school year. The total population is expected to shift to a low of 78 students in the 2027-2028 school 
year to a possible high of 103 students in the 2030-2031 school year. Bear in mind that the PSD 
Mountain School enrollment is very sensitive to shifts in population in the mountain communities. 
Due to the small enrollment, there are three combo classes at each Mt. School with one teacher 
assigned each to K/1, 2/3, and 4/5.
