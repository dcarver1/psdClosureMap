B. Implementation Criteria (Operational Viability & Transition Management) 

4. Traffic, Safety, and Walkability: Management of vehicular traffic flow, pedestrian safety, and 
neighborhood walkability for students and families. 

• 
Increased traffic is expected around Bennett and Lopez with the increased enrollment; however, no 
structural traffic mitigation measures are recommended. 

• 
PSD and the City of Fort Collins recognize there will be increased delays around the school during 
15-minute peak periods at pick-up and drop-off times. 

• 
Parent pickup traffic at Bennett is adjacent to the bus zone causing increased congestion. 

• 
Parent pickup traffic at Lopez is located on Benthaven St and Wabash St minimizing congestion. 

• 
Bennett is within the City of Fort Collins and has ample curb space and sidewalk connectivity within 
walk boundaries. Closest SRO (FCPS at Blevins) assigned is 1.9 miles from site. Closest fire station 
(PFA Station 2) is 1.4 miles from school. 

• 
Lopez is within the City of Fort Collins and has ample curb space and sidewalk connectivity within 
walk boundaries. Closest SRO (FCPS at Webber) assigned is 1.3 miles from site. Closest fire station 
(PFA Station 5) is 1.8 miles from school. 

 

5. Transportation & Commute Length: Availability of district transportation routes and total commute 
length for affected students. 
• 
157 students that previously walked to Beattie will be eligible for busing to their 
neighborhood school Lopez or Bennett. 

• 
Average student ride times to Beattie currently: 

o Average – 16 minutes 
o Max ride time – 26 minutes 

• 
Anticipated student ride times: 

o Average – 15 minutes 
o Max ride time – 33 minutes 

 

6. Maximization of School Program Offerings: Preservation and expansion of Specials (art, music, PE), 
electives, extracurriculars/athletics, early childhood programming, and before/after-school care. 
• 
We project Lopez and Bennett to have 3-4 tracks at each grade level. This will facilitate full-time or 
nearly full-time specials at each school. 

• 
Before and after school care can be accommodated at the receiving site. 

• 
We are projecting that early childhood programming would remain at both Lopez and Bennett.
