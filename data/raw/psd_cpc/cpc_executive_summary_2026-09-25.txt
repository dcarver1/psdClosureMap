Summary Rationale 

Multiple scenarios were evaluated for the 5 southwest elementary schools (Beattie, Johnson, Lopez, McGraw, 
Olander). Beattie Elementary ranked 5th in the region on composite z-scores. This was largely skewed by the 
facility condition assessment of adding air conditioning and the facility size of Beattie being one of the smaller 
schools in the region which increases its’ utilization percentile. Due to physical size constraints and floor plan, 
the committee did not support Beattie being a receiving school in any scenario and believed its' current and 
future enrollment needed to be addressed to improve the educational programming. The implementation 
criteria evaluation elevated two areas of additional concern about Beattie. It was noted to be a more 
challenging receiver site for intensive center-based programs due to the open floor plan and needing to create 
secure spaces for more significantly impacted students. Additionally, Beattie's open floor plan was noted as a 
more at-risk environment to secure in a school lockdown. After reviewing the identification criteria, 
implementation analysis, and equity analysis, the CPC determined that Beattie would be recommended due to 
its' current and projected enrollment, low utilization, facility design, proximity to other elementary schools, and 
how its' closure contributes to positively providing greater stability and programmatic opportunities for all 
students in the region. 

Financial Considerations: 
Cost for overall day-to-day operation could decrease by $1.89 million (includes estimate for Johnson closure as 
well). This includes changes to student-based budget, school administration, custodial, child nutrition, and 
utilities.

Summary Rationale 

Irish Elementary has experienced continuously declining enrollment. Student enrollment at Irish Elementary is 
projected at 251 students in the 2026-2027 school year and is expected to remain in continuous decline for the 
next five years losing up to 84 students, 30.8%, by the 2030-2031 school year. At this time, we expect a funded 
enrollment of approximately 188 students during the 2030-2031 school year. This is less than a two-track school. 

Irish’s current utilization is under 50%. 

Putnam and Irish were considered for combining, but overall west side enrollment and utilization as well as 
student programming are improved by both schools closing rather than consolidating. Within the equity 
analysis, closing Irish and Putnam was also affirmed as a preferred scenario over consolidating to create more 
diverse and less segregated communities by language, culture, and socioeconomic status. Research supports 
that more integrated school environments produce better short term and long-term effects for children. To 
mitigate the loss of the Dual Language Program at Irish, a specialized transition plan for multilingual learners has 
been created to ensure continuity of access to DL for the students that need the program the most. This 
scenario enables students to receive access to more extra-curriculars, programming, and more full-time staff by 
attending Bauder/CLPE. Additionally, this scenario improves the utilization and enrollment of Cache La Poudre 
Elementary and Bauder Elementary. 

There is no center-based program at Irish, so continuity of programming for students served with a significant 
IEP will not be disrupted. Continuity of dual language programming will be addressed through possible 
integration of students at Harris Elementary or a future expansion of dual-language programming at a single 
site. 

Financial Considerations: Cost for overall day-to-day operation could decrease by $1.91 million. This assumes an 
Irish and Putnam closure. This includes changes to student-based budget, school administration, custodial, child 
nutrition, and utilities.

 
The current expectation is that the need for our AEC campuses with their current programming will 
stay relatively consistent over the next 5 years. 
 
Centennial High School and Poudre Community Academy are classified by the Colorado Department 
of Education as Alternative Education Campuses (AEC). Enrollment at our AEC campuses is 
conducted via placement or referral. These schools do not have a residence boundary in the same 
manner as a neighborhood school does. Enrollment is dictated by the needs of our students. 
 
With that in mind, Centennial High School had an enrollment of 125 students in the 2021-2022 
school year. The population oscillated up and down to a high of 138 students in the 2024-2025 
school year and a low of 100 students in the 2025-2026 school year, a net decline of 25 students 
over a 5-year period, 20.0%.  Centennial High School is expected to maintain an average enrollment 
of approximately 102 students over the next 5 years. 
 
The enrollment at Poudre Community Academy was 235 students in 2021-2022. The population 
oscillated up and down from the high in 2021-2022 to a low of 93 students in 2025-2026, a net 
decline of 142 students over a 5-year period, 60.4%. 
 
Poudre Community Academy has had a diverse portfolio of additional programs associated with its 
state school code over the years including our Expelled School, Special Education Transition 
Programs, and HSED Programs. The projections for Transition Programs and HSED programming are 
separate from the Poudre Community Academy. Also, the district expelled school program, the 
Journey Program, which is utilized as needed by our community, is not projected.  
 
The Poudre Community Academy school is expected to maintain an average enrollment of 
approximately 90 students over the next 5 years.

Core Principles & Equity Integration 

Policy FCB-R states that all recommendations regarding school closure, consolidation, and/or relocation reflect 
both educational quality and fiscal stewardship values across Poudre School District: 

Totality of Circumstances: Evaluation criteria are analyzed in no particular order. Equity is evaluated not as a 
separate standalone criterion, but as an essential factor considered as part of the totality of the circumstances, 
guided by administrative equity standards. 

Educational Optimization: Decisions prioritize maintaining and expanding equitable access to high-quality 
instruction, specialized programming, necessary support services, and co-curricular offerings. 

Stakeholder Engagement & Procedural Timeline Requirements 

Community & Staff Engagement dates and times: The Superintendent holds public meetings to gather input and 
adapt recommendations as appropriate from staff, families and caregivers.  These meetings have been 
scheduled and are not open to the general public.  The Board of Education will hold additional listening sessions 
in the community before a public vote.
